Discover Sri Lanka

Start with three places to explore in and around Kandy.

Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic

Visit Sri Dalada Maligawa, the Buddhist temple that houses the Sacred Tooth Relic. The temple’s architecture and daily rituals make it a central stop in Kandy.

See the tour ↗

Royal Botanical Gardens, Peradeniya

Explore palm avenues, orchids, bamboo and lawns just outside Kandy. The gardens are a stop on AMAK’s tuk-tuk itinerary.

See the tour ↗

Kandy Lake

Take a lakeside walk in the heart of the city. The lake dates to the reign of Sri Wickrama Rajasinghe and sits beside the Temple of the Tooth.
