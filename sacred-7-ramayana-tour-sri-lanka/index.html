Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 1Chilaw

    Visit Munneswaram and Manavari after airport pickup.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  2. Day 2Sigiriya

    Visit Lion Rock with an optional stop at Dambulla.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  3. Day 3Kandy

    See the Temple of the Tooth and ask about a cultural performance.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  4. Day 4Ramboda and Nuwara Eliya

    Visit the Hanuman Temple, Ramboda Falls and Seetha Amman Temple.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  5. Day 5Ella

    Visit Divurumpola, Ravana Falls and the Nine Arch Bridge. Cave access depends on conditions.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  6. Day 6Kataragama and the south coast

    Continue via Kataragama and Rumassala to the Galle area.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  7. Day 7Galle and Colombo

    Explore the fort and visit Panchamuga Anjaneyar Temple in Colombo.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  8. Day 8Departure or extension

    Transfer to the airport or ask about extra time on the coast.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
