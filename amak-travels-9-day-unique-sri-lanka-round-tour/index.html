Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 1Bentota

    Meet your driver at the airport and head to the coast for water activities and a turtle hatchery visit.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  2. Day 2Ratnapura

    Visit a gemstone mine and explore Diva Guhava cave.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  3. Day 3Matara

    Take a river safari and visit Blue Beach Island for sunset.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  4. Day 4Gal Oya

    Join a guided Vedda village walk and an evening boat safari.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  5. Day 5Gal Oya National Park

    Spend the morning hiking and head out again for a night wildlife drive.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  6. Day 6Maskeliya and Gartmore

    Visit a tea estate and Gartmore Waterfall.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  7. Day 7Kandy

    See Puna Ella and visit Ambuluwawa Tower.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  8. Day 8Sigiriya

    Visit the Temple of the Tooth and Meeyan Ella on the way to Sigiriya.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  9. Day 9Sigiriya, Colombo and departure

    Climb Lion Rock in the morning, then stop in Colombo before the airport transfer.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
