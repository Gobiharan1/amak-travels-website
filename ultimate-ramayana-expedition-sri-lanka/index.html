Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 1Arrival and Colombo temples

    Begin with a Colombo welcome and visits to Panchamuga Anjaneyar and Kelaniya.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  2. Day 2Chilaw and Puttalam coast

    Visit Munneswaram and Manavari. Ask about seasonal options near Kalpitiya.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  3. Day 3Mannar and Ram Setu viewpoints

    See the approved viewpoints near Talaimannar and explore Mannar’s heritage.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  4. Day 4Jaffna heritage

    Visit Nallur Kandaswamy Temple, Keerimalai and Jaffna Fort.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  5. Day 5Jaffna to Anuradhapura

    Travel from Jaffna to Anuradhapura via Thiruketheeswaram. Ask about Ritigala access.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  6. Day 6Sigiriya and Habarana

    Explore Sigiriya and the surrounding Cultural Triangle.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  7. Day 7Trincomalee and Koneswaram

    Visit Koneswaram Temple and spend time near the coast around Nilaveli.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  8. Day 8Dunuwila, Lakegala and Knuckles

    Continue inland via Dunuwila and the Knuckles region, where conditions allow.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  9. Day 9Kandy cultural day

    Visit the Temple of the Tooth, Peradeniya gardens and Kandy Lake.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  10. Day 10Ramboda and Nuwara Eliya

    Follow the hill-country route through Ramboda to the Seetha Amman Temple area.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  11. Day 11Hill-country traditions

    Explore the Pussellawa and Kotmale area. Specialist access depends on the route and conditions.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  12. Day 12Ella and the Ravana sites

    Visit Divurumpola and Ravana Falls. Ask AMAK which cave visits are suitable for your group.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  13. Day 13Kataragama, Ussangoda and Galle

    Travel south via Kataragama and Ussangoda before continuing toward Galle.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  14. Day 14Galle to Colombo

    Visit Galle Fort and continue to Colombo.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
  15. Day 15Departure or extension

    Transfer to the airport or ask about extra time on the coast or an additional wildlife stop.

    Find this stop on the map ↗
