Refund and cancellation policy

AMAK Travels booking and customer information.

Booking confirmation

AMAK confirms bookings once it receives confirmation of payment.

Customer cancellations

The published policy lists a full refund at seven days or more before the tour, 50% at three to six days, and no refund at less than 48 hours. Check the terms in your confirmed quote for your package.

No-shows and operator cancellations

The published policy gives no refund for a no-show. When AMAK cancels for weather, safety or unavoidable circumstances, it offers a full refund or rescheduling.

Refund processing

The policy gives a processing window of seven to fourteen business days for approved refunds through the original payment method.
