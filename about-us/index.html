About AMAK Travels

Meet the Sri Lankan team behind the tours.

A tour operator based in Sri Lanka’s Central Province

AMAK Travels plans private and tailor-made journeys across Sri Lanka. The team’s roots in the Central Province shape its tours around Kandy, the tea estates, waterfalls and historic temples.

Travel at your own pace

Choose a cultural day trip, a guided hike or an island-wide round tour. The team helps with itinerary planning and local guidance, taking your interests, schedule and group size into account.

A local approach

AMAK aims to make travel comfortable and personal while respecting the people, places and landscapes guests come to experience.
